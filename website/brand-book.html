02 — Signature

A useful gesture, made visible

Back to where
you already are.

A browser tab with an arrow returning inside. The wordmark is tabhero.: lowercase, with a full stop to end the detour. In running text, write TabHero.

Primary — light background. SVG lettering uses outlines, with no installed font required.

Monochrome — reproduction in one ink.

On ink — cream lettering and lime symbol.

A little breathing room

Leave at least ¼ of the symbol’s height on every side (x). Prefer the symbol alone when the full signature would be narrower than 180 px; minimum symbol size: 24 px. Check legibility at the final size.

Keep proportions, lettering, and colors intact. Don’t stretch, tilt, add shadows to the asset, or combine it with third-party logos. No phone, speech bubble, or official-looking badge.

Use ink text on cream, lime, or coral. On ink, prefer cream or lime. Color helps organize; instructions and states also need words. Check contrast in the final application, including keyboard focus.

04 — Voice

A gentle nudge. No personal digs.

Talk like
someone who uses it.

Warm, direct, and a little irreverent. Make the joke about the extra tab; explain what happens to the chat. When something fails, trade the joke for a useful next step.

Recognize the friction

“Another tab? It’s already open.”

The humor is in the repeated detour. No insults and no claims that everyone has the same experience.
Explain the outcome

“New chat.
Same tab.”

Show the draft and manual sending. Clearly label any demonstration that uses a simulated interface.
Keep the idea open

“The best version of TabHero?
The one you never have to install.”

A hope for native adoption, not an acquisition pitch or a claim of interest from Meta. MIT permits reuse under its terms, with no obligation to adopt or acquire the project.
Optional support

“Like it? Support it from US$ 1.”

GitHub Sponsors. Always optional. Receiving payments is not enabled yet. When available, confirm the amount and payment type on GitHub; never imply a one-time payment when it is recurring.

On failure: “I couldn’t open the chat. Your tab is still as you left it.” Use that message only when the implementation guarantees that behavior.

No invented consensus · No manufactured urgency04 / 05

Show it. Explain it. Let people inspect it.

Open code.
Open about limits.
